**[PAID] 3D character artist: four stylised cartoon monkeys for a social bluffing game (Blender → glTF)**

We are a small indie team making **Üç Maymun** ("Three Monkeys"), a four-player online bluffing game for PC/Steam, built in Godot 4.

**The game:**
- Four monkeys sit around a card table in a smoky 1970s Istanbul back room.
- They lie about how much money they made, audit each other, cover up for friends, and show off the accessories they buy.
- The characters carry the game. Players read each other's faces, so the monkeys need big, readable personalities.

**Style:**
- Stylised cartoon, bold silhouettes, exaggerated expressions, warm colours.
- Think readable from across a table on a stream, not realistic.
- References: [link to our reference board].

**What we need, in milestones:**
1. **Concept:** front and side designs of four monkeys (one family, clearly different from each other: fur, outfit, colour, attitude), their accessories and the auditor's hat.
2. **One base monkey, game-ready:**
   - model and textures;
   - humanoid rig;
   - mouth (`jaw_open`) and blink shape keys;
   - five accessories (glasses, gloves, watch, gold chain, crown) and the auditor's eyeshade.

   We put it in the game and decide on the next steps.
3. **Five seated animations:** idle, talk, caught (panic), cheer, slump.
4. **Three more monkeys** on the same base and rig.

**Technical spec:** a short document with names, scale, polygon budget (≤ 20k triangles with accessories), export settings and the table's measurements.
- We also send a free test monkey built to the spec. Open it in Blender to see every name.
- Every delivery goes through our automatic checker, so you get instant, precise feedback.

**Budget:** [USD 1,000–2,000] for milestones 1–2; milestones 3–4 quoted separately. Paid per accepted milestone (platform escrow is fine).

**Timeline:** about [8–12] weeks for everything, milestones 1–2 in the first [4–6].

**Rights:** full commercial rights transfer on payment (work for hire). You can show the work in your portfolio once the game is announced, and you are credited in the game.

**Please send:**
- 2–3 game-ready, rigged characters you made: wireframe, polycount, a turntable or a Sketchfab link. Stylised work with faces and expressions especially.
- Your price per milestone, revisions included, and when you could start.
- Whether you use AI tools anywhere in your process (Steam requires us to disclose AI content).
